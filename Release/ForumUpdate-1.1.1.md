**Realistic Volumetric Aurorae 1.1.1 is up** (SpaceDock, GitHub; CKAN follows).

A fix release for something a few of you will have seen from Mun orbit: Kerbin's aurora
showing through the Mun's ground just below the horizon, and with the camera zoomed all the
way out, where KSP swaps the Mun's terrain for its smooth distant model, the aurora coming
back under the ridges on the limb. The plugin now builds its own model of the body you are
at from the terrain's height function, so the aurora hides behind the real highlands at any
altitude. The maps for the stock bodies ship with the mod; a planet pack's bodies build
theirs the first time you are there (about twenty seconds, once). The aurora fading in and
out with the camera angle around an airless body is fixed too.

Install over 1.1.0; your settings file is left alone. If you are still on 1.0.x, 1.1.0's
notes (Jool, Laythe, the light on the vessel, the storms) are in the changelog.
