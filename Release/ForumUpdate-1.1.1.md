**1.1.1 is up** (SpaceDock, GitHub, CKAN will follow).

Fix release for something a few of you probably saw from Mun orbit: Kerbin's aurora showing
through the Muns ground just below the horizon, and with the camera zoomed all the way out
(where KSP swaps the terrain for the smooth distant model) the aurora coming back under the
ridges on the limb. The plugin now builds its own model of the body your at from the terrain
height function so the aurora hides behind the real highlands at any altitude. Maps for the
stock bodies ship with the mod, planet pack bodies build theirs the first time your there
(about 20 seconds, once). The aurora fading in and out with camera angle around an airless
body is fixed too.

Install over 1.1.0, your settings file is left alone. If your still on 1.0.x the 1.1.0 notes
(Jool, Laythe, vessel light, storms) are in the changelog.
